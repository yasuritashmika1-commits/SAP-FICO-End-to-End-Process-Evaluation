# SAP FICO Process Improvements

## Proposed Enhancements

### 1. Process Standardization

Develop standard operating procedures for all major SAP FI and CO processes.

### 2. Master Data Governance

Introduce approval and validation procedures for important master data.

### 3. Automated Workflows

Automate selected approval processes.

### 4. Improved Reporting

Develop reports for:

- Cost center performance
- Planned versus actual costs
- Internal orders
- Accounts receivable
- Accounts payable

### 5. User Training

Provide regular SAP training and support.
